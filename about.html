Our mission

About Manjushree Biotech

Manjushree Biotech is a Nepal-based biotechnology company. We supply laboratory products and guide laboratories to accreditation today, while building toward molecular research and diagnostic product development.

What we believe

Quality is the connecting thread

Whatever we're doing — supplying a reagent, writing a Quality Manual, or eventually developing a diagnostic product — the standard is the same: laboratory-grade rigor, applied practically.

Practical over theoretical

Solutions built for how a laboratory actually runs, not just what a standard says on paper.

Built on real laboratory experience

Our approach to trading and consultation is grounded in hands-on laboratory and diagnostics work.

Growing deliberately

Trading and consultation fund and inform the research capability we're building, rather than the other way around.
